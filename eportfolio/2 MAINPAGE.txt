<h2>🔥 Reflection</h2>
<hr>
<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
Buwan ng Wika:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from the event is to love and cherish our culture and never forget how important and special it is. I can apply this by speaking our Filipino language proudly and showing respect for our traditions. I’ve actively participated in this event by showing respect to our country and heroes, like honoring the flag and being kind to everyone. I also joined some Filipino performances that were part of Buwan ng Wika. If I were to teach this topic to a classmate, I’d say that Buwan ng Wika helps us remember who we are as Filipinos and teaches us to be proud of our language. It’s important to have this event because it reminds us to love our country and keep our culture alive.
</p>
<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
Intrams:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from Intrams is teamwork and cooperation. I can apply this in real-life situations through helping my friends, classmates, and family when we work together or play games. I actively participated in the event through joining games, cheering for my team, and showing good sportsmanship. If I were to teach this topic to my classmates, I’d tell them that Intrams is not just about winning, but about playing fair and supporting each other. It’s important to have this event because it teaches us unity, friendship, and how to enjoy sports while respecting everyone.
</p>
<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
Science month:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from Science Month is that science helps us understand how things work and how to take care of our health and our planet. I can apply this in real life by keeping myself clean, saving water, and helping others learn about good habits. I participated in the event by making posters, helping with our wins corner, and joining other activities like the toothbrushing and proper hygiene events. If I were to teach this topic to my classmates, I’d say that science is fun and useful because it teaches us to explore and take care of everything around us. It’s important to have this event because it reminds us that science is a big part of our everyday life and it makes learning more exciting.</p>

<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
AP month:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from AP Month is that learning about history and different countries helps us understand people and cultures better.
I can apply this in real life by respecting others and learning more about what makes every country special. 
Our AP Month hasn’t really started yet, but I can already say that it’s going to be fun, especially the upcoming United Nations event because it will help us learn about other nations and how we can all work together. 
If I were to teach this topic to my classmates, I’d say that AP Month is about learning from the past and appreciating every culture. 
It’s important to have this event because it teaches us to value history, respect differences, and be proud of our identity.</p>

<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
Teachers day:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from Teachers’ Day is to always appreciate and thank our teachers for all the things they do for us. I can apply this in real life by being respectful, listening to my teachers, and always doing my best in school. I participated in the event by joining my classmates in giving small tokens of appreciation to our teachers and bonding with them through fun activities. If I were to teach this topic to my classmates, I’d say that Teachers’ Day is a special time to make our teachers feel loved and valued. It’s important to have this event because it reminds us that our teachers work hard to help us learn and grow every day.</p>

<p><span style="color:#ff2d2d;font-weight:900;font-family:'Montserrat',sans-serif;text-shadow:1px 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5);letter-spacing:1px;">
Cluster meet:
</span><br>
The most important thing I’ve learned from the Cluster Meet is that teamwork and sportsmanship are really important, not just in games but also in real life. I can apply this by always supporting others and being a good sport, even if I’m not the one playing. Even though I wasn’t a player, I enjoyed watching every team’s cooperation and cheering for them. I also made new friends from other schools, which made the event even more fun. If I were to teach this topic to my classmates, I’d say that Cluster Meet isn’t just about winning but about learning to work together and make new friends. It’s important to have this event because it builds unity, friendship, and school pride.</p>
